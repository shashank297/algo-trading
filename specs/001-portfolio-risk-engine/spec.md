# Feature Specification: Portfolio Risk Engine

**Feature Branch**: `[001-portfolio-risk-engine]`

**Created**: 2026-08-19

**Status**: Draft

**Input**: User description: "Add a centralized portfolio risk engine to the existing trading platform. The existing strategy and agent modules must remain compatible. Requirements: - maximum position size - maximum portfolio exposure - maximum sector exposure - stop-loss controls - daily loss limit - maximum drawdown protection - pre-trade validation - risk rejection reasons - audit trail. The risk engine must sit between strategy decisions and trade execution."

## User Scenarios & Testing *(mandatory)*

### User Story 1 - Pre-trade validation of a compliant trade (Priority: P1)

As a strategy execution flow, I want my valid trade intents to pass through the risk engine seamlessly so that compliant trades are executed without interruption.

**Why this priority**: Core path; the risk engine must allow valid business logic to proceed to execution.

**Independent Test**: Can be fully tested by submitting a trade intent well within all risk limits and observing that it is passed to the execution module.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:
1. **Given** a portfolio well under its maximum exposure limit, **When** a strategy submits a normal-sized trade intent, **Then** the risk engine approves the trade and forwards it to execution.

---

### User Story 2 - Position and Portfolio Exposure Limits (Priority: P1)

As a portfolio manager, I want the risk engine to reject trades that would exceed maximum position size or total portfolio exposure, so that the fund is protected from over-concentration and over-leverage.

**Why this priority**: Preventing excessive capital allocation to a single asset or overall portfolio is the most fundamental risk control.

**Independent Test**: Can be fully tested by submitting a trade intent that breaches configured limits and verifying it is rejected with a clear reason.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:
1. **Given** a maximum position size of 5%, **When** a strategy attempts to allocate 10% to a single asset, **Then** the risk engine rejects the trade intent with a "Position Size Limit Exceeded" reason.
2. **Given** a maximum portfolio exposure of 100%, **When** an incoming trade would push total exposure to 105%, **Then** the risk engine rejects the trade intent.

---

### User Story 3 - Drawdown and Daily Loss Protection (Priority: P1)

As a portfolio manager, I want the risk engine to halt trading if the portfolio hits its daily loss limit or maximum drawdown threshold, so that catastrophic losses are prevented.

**Why this priority**: Circuit breakers are critical for protecting capital during extreme market volatility or strategy malfunction.

**Independent Test**: Can be fully tested by artificially setting the portfolio state to exceed the drawdown limit and verifying all subsequent opening trades are rejected.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:
1. **Given** a daily loss limit of 2%, **When** the portfolio is down 2.1% for the day and a new opening trade is proposed, **Then** the risk engine rejects the trade.
2. **Given** a maximum drawdown threshold of 10%, **When** the portfolio equity drops 11% below its peak, **Then** the risk engine rejects all new risk-increasing trades.

---

### User Story 4 - Sector Exposure Enforcement (Priority: P2)

As a portfolio manager, I want the risk engine to monitor and limit the total capital allocated to any single market sector to ensure adequate diversification.

**Why this priority**: Sector diversification reduces correlation risk, but is slightly secondary to absolute position sizing.

**Independent Test**: Can be fully tested by attempting to overweight a specific sector beyond its limit and verifying rejection.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:
1. **Given** a maximum sector exposure of 20%, **When** a trade intent would push the "Technology" sector exposure to 25%, **Then** the risk engine rejects the trade intent.

---

### Edge Cases

- What happens when a trade intent is to *reduce* risk (e.g., closing a position) while the portfolio is in a drawdown state? (The engine should approve risk-reducing trades).
- How does the system handle sector exposure limits if an instrument's sector metadata is missing? (It should fall back to an "Unknown" sector bucket and enforce limits there).

## Requirements *(mandatory)*

### Functional Requirements

- **FR-001**: The risk engine MUST intercept all trade decisions generated by strategies before they reach the trade execution module.
- **FR-002**: The risk engine MUST evaluate trade intents against a configured maximum position size constraint.
- **FR-003**: The risk engine MUST reject trade intents that exceed the maximum total portfolio exposure limit.
- **FR-004**: The risk engine MUST track and enforce maximum sector exposure across all open positions.
- **FR-005**: The risk engine MUST support setting and evaluating stop-loss controls for individual positions.
- **FR-006**: The risk engine MUST enforce a daily loss limit across the entire portfolio.
- **FR-007**: The risk engine MUST enforce maximum drawdown protection (peak-to-trough decline).
- **FR-008**: The risk engine MUST return structured, readable risk rejection reasons when a trade is blocked.
- **FR-009**: The risk engine MUST persist a detailed audit trail of all evaluated decisions and outcomes.
- **FR-010**: The integration of the risk engine MUST NOT require any modifications to existing strategy or agent logic modules.

### Key Entities

- **Trade Intent**: A proposed order generated by a strategy, pending risk approval.
- **Risk Evaluation Result**: The outcome of the risk engine's checks, containing a boolean approval status and a list of rejection reasons.
- **Risk Configuration**: The set of defined thresholds (max position, max drawdown, etc.) applied to the portfolio.

## Success Criteria *(mandatory)*

### Measurable Outcomes

- **SC-001**: 100% of generated trade decisions are routed through the centralized risk engine.
- **SC-002**: Trade decisions violating any risk constraint are rejected instantly with a clearly identifiable reason.
- **SC-003**: Existing strategies and agents continue to function with 0 lines of code changed in their core logic files.
- **SC-004**: 100% of risk evaluations (both approvals and rejections) are stored and queryable in an audit trail.

## Assumptions

- The existing storage layer (DuckDB) will be used to persist the audit trail.
- Instrument sector metadata is available or can be statically mapped for sector exposure checks.
- Market data (equity curves, daily P&L) is readily accessible to the risk engine to calculate drawdowns and daily loss limits.
- Risk-reducing trades (closing positions, tightening stop-losses) are generally allowed even if the portfolio is violating a limit (e.g., in a drawdown state).
