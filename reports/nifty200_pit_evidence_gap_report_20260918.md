## Executive Summary

A comprehensive forensic investigation was conducted across official National Stock Exchange (NSE) of India and Nifty Indices public endpoints to resolve the 1,121 Point-in-Time (PIT) evidence blockers recorded in `artifacts/nifty200_pit_v1/blocker_ledger.csv`.

Under strict platform rules, no synthetic initial membership was created, no ISINs were inferred from company names, no fuzzy matching was used, B1 rows were restricted to search indexing only, and non-authoritative candidate tables (`symbolchange.csv`, `namechange.csv`) were preserved as candidates without promoting them to certified identity links.

As established by first-party evidence probing, free authoritative evidence does not exist in the public domain to close the initial membership anchor, monthly weightage gaps, or identity-lacking historical corporate-action logs. Consequently, the pipeline fails closed as designed.
